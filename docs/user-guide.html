1 Getting Started

1.1 Registration

Navigate to /register to create your account. The page presents a split-screen layout with platform branding on the left and the registration form on the right.

1
Full Name — Enter your first and last name as it should appear on invoices and firm records.
2
Firm Email — Use your professional firm email address. This will be your login identifier.
3
Password — At least 6 characters. Use a combination of letters, numbers, and symbols.
4
Confirm Password — Re-enter your password. The system validates both entries match.
5
Click Create Account. If email confirmation is enabled, you will be redirected to login with a banner: “Account created. Check your email for a confirmation link.”

2 Initial Setup

Before creating clients and matters, configure your firm profile by navigating to Settings (gear icon in the sidebar).

2.1 Firm Profile

The Firm Profile tab controls how your firm appears on invoices and documents.

FieldDescription
Firm NameYour law firm’s legal name. Displayed on invoices and documents.
Default Billable RateStandard hourly rate ($/hr) applied to new time entries and matters by default.
TimezoneYour operating timezone for date/time displays (EST, CST, MST, PST).
Date FormatChoose MM/DD/YYYY, DD/MM/YYYY, or YYYY-MM-DD.
AddressFirm address shown on invoices. Use the AI Text Assistant (sparkle button) to help format it.
Phone / Email / WebsiteFirm contact information for invoice headers.
Firm LogoUpload your firm’s logo. Appears at the top of invoices and PDF exports. Max 5 MB.
Billing Cycle (Days)How often auto-invoices are generated (default 30).
Billing Lead Time (Days)Days before cycle end to generate draft invoices (default 5).
📷
Screenshot: Firm Profile Settings

The Firm Profile form with Firm Name, Default Billable Rate, Timezone selector, Date Format selector, Address textarea (with AI sparkle button), Phone, Email, Website, Logo upload area, and Billing Cycle fields.

Important: Set your Default Billable Rate and Firm Name before creating time entries or invoices. These values propagate to all new records automatically.

2.2 Rate Cards

Custom billing rates override the default rate for specific clients or matters. Navigate to Settings → Rate Cards.

1
Click Add Rate Card.
2
Enter the Rate ($/hr), a descriptive Label (e.g., “Corporate Preferred Rate”), and optionally link it to a Client and/or Matter.
3
Set Effective From and To dates for time-limited rates.
4
Click Save.
Rate resolution order: The system applies rates in this priority: Matter-specific rate → Client-specific rate → Firm default rate. The most specific match wins.

2.3 Billing Cycle Configuration

Configured in Settings → Firm Profile:

  • Billing Cycle Days — Set to 30 for monthly billing, 15 for bi-monthly, etc. The system checks for unbilled time entries at this interval.
  • Billing Lead Time — Draft invoices are generated this many days before the cycle ends, giving you time to review before sending.

You can also override the billing cycle per matter from the matter edit page for clients with custom billing arrangements.

3 Client Management

3.1 Creating a Client (Wizard)

Navigate to Clients in the sidebar and click the New Client button. This opens a 5-step onboarding wizard.

📷
Screenshot: Client Directory

The Client Directory grid with the “New Client” button at top-right and “Import CSV” button next to it. Search bar and sort controls are visible above the client cards.

Step 1: Company Information

  • Client Name (required) — Company or individual name.
  • Industry — e.g., Technology, Healthcare, Financial Services.
  • Status — Active (default), Inactive, or Prospect.
  • Registration Number, Tax ID, VAT Number — Official business identifiers.
  • Business Type — LLC, Corporation, Partnership, Sole Proprietorship, Non-Profit, Other.
  • Date of Incorporation (YYYY-MM-DD), Jurisdiction, Website, Tags (comma-separated).
📷
Screenshot: New Client Wizard — Step 1 (Company Info)

Step 1 of the wizard with fields for Client Name, Industry, Status dropdown, Registration Number, Tax ID, VAT Number, Business Type dropdown, Date of Incorporation, Jurisdiction, Website, and Tags. A step indicator shows Step 1 of 5 highlighted.

Step 2: Contact & Address

  • Contact Name, Contact Title, Email, Phone.
  • Registered Address — Street, City, State, ZIP, Country.
  • Billing Address — Same-as toggle to auto-copy from registered address.
  • Internal Notes with AI Text Assistant support.

Step 3: Financial Details

  • Annual Revenue Range, Employee Count.
  • Billing Terms — Net 15, Net 30, Net 45, Net 60, Net 90, or Due on Receipt.
  • Credit Limit, Referral Source.
  • Internal Notes with AI Text Assistant.

Step 4: Matter Creation

Create one or more matters during onboarding. At least one matter with a title and practice area is required.

  • Matter Title (required), Matter Code (auto-generated or custom).
  • Practice Area (required) — Corporate, Litigation, IP, Real Estate.
  • Lead Attorney, Description (with AI Text Assistant).

Step 5: Document Upload

Upload client documents with labels (Certificate of Incorporation, Tax Document, Contract, etc.). This step is optional.

3.2 Bulk Import via CSV

For adding multiple clients at once, use the CSV import feature from the Client Directory.

1
Click Import CSV at the top of the Client Directory page.
2
Download the CSV Template via the link in the modal. The template includes 31 columns matching the registration form.
3
Fill in your client data in the CSV. Only Name is required. Refer to the modal’s “Accepted values reference” for Status, Business Type, and Billing Terms options.
4
Drag and drop your CSV file onto the upload area, or click to browse.
5
Review the preview table. Invalid rows (missing Name) are flagged. Only valid rows will be imported.
6
Click Import N Clients. A results table shows which clients were created and any errors encountered.
📷
Screenshot: CSV Import Modal

The modal showing a template download link, a drag-and-drop upload area, a preview table with row validation statuses (checkmark or warning icon), and the “Import Clients” button. The “Accepted values reference” accordion is expanded at the bottom.

CSV format notes: Addresses use separate columns (Street, City, State, Zip, Country) for both registered and billing addresses. Tags are comma-separated. Dates use YYYY-MM-DD format. Enum fields must match the accepted values shown in the modal reference.

3.3 Client Directory

The Client Directory displays all your clients in a sortable, searchable grid (12 per page).

  • Search by name, contact name, or email.
  • Sort by Name, Active Matters, or Created Date (ascending/descending).
  • Each card shows: client name, status badge, contact info, active matters count, balance, and action buttons (Matters, Invoice, Document).
  • Click a client card to view details, edit, manage matters, and upload documents.

5 Time Tracking

Kiya Law offers three methods for logging time, each suited to different workflows.

5.1 Manual Full Entry

Navigate to Time → Manual Entry (/time/new) for detailed time logging.

1
Select Client from the dropdown. Matters filter automatically based on the selected client.
2
Select the Matter this time entry belongs to.
3
Choose a Task Category: Drafting & Revision, Client Consultation, Legal Research, Court Appearance.
4
Set the Date and Duration (hours and minutes).
5
Narrative Description (required) — Detailed account of legal services rendered. Use the AI Text Assistant to refine.
6
Internal Notes — Non-billable context for internal reference.
7
Toggle Billable on or off. Click Save Entry.
📷
Screenshot: Manual Time Entry Form

The full form showing Client and Matter dropdowns, Task Category selector, Date picker, Duration fields (hours and minutes), Narrative Description textarea (with AI sparkle), Internal Notes textarea (with AI sparkle), and Billable toggle. A back navigation link and “Save Entry” button are at the top and bottom.

5.2 Quick Entry

From the main Time page (/time), the left panel offers a streamlined quick entry form using useActionState for fast submission.

  • Select Client and Matter from grouped dropdowns.
  • Enter Date, Hours, and a Description (with AI Text Assistant).
  • Click Log Time Entry. The form resets for the next entry automatically on success.

5.3 AI Natural Language Logger

Navigate to AI Time Logger (/time/ai-logger) for the fastest time entry method.

1
Type a plain English description of your work in the large text input. Example: “1.5h call with Oracle rep regarding compliance framework review” or “Drafted 10-page M&A agreement for NovaTech.”
2
Click the Magic Wand (sparkle) button. The AI parses your text into a structured time entry: description, hours, matter, client name, and date.
3
Review the parsed result in the review card. Verify the description, matched matter, hours, and date.
4
Click Add to Log to save, or Discard to cancel.
📷
Screenshot: AI Time Logger

A large natural language input field with a sparkle button. Below it, a parsed result card showing the extracted description, matched matter name, hours, date, and buttons for “Add to Log” and “Discard.” A “Today’s Log” section at the bottom shows current entries and daily totals.

Best results with AI Logger: Include the client or matter name, approximate hours, and a brief description of the work. Clear, specific language produces the best parses. Examples: “2h drafting shareholder agreement for Vertex Capital” or “0.5h reviewing court filing for HealthPlus litigation matter.”

7.2 Creating an Invoice

Navigate to Billing → Create New Invoice or click the gold “Create Invoice” button in the sidebar.

1
Select Client — Opens the Select Client modal. The client’s billing address, email, and phone auto-fill.
2
Select Matter — Filtered by the selected client. Choose the matter being billed.
3
Invoice Number — Auto-generated. Customizable.
4
Invoice Date and Due Date — Defaults to today and +30 days.
5
Line Items — Add rows with Description, Hours, and Rate. Total calculates automatically. Use + to add rows, trash icon to remove.
6
Notes & Payment Terms — Optional section for custom terms, bank details, or instructions. AI Text Assistant available.
7
Click Save & Issue Invoice. The invoice is created in Draft status and appears in the billing list.

7.3 Invoice Detail & Actions

Click any invoice in the billing list to view its detail page. Features and actions include:

  • Download PDF — Generates a professional PDF matching the on-screen layout, including firm logo, stamp, and all details.
  • Status Badge — Color-coded status indicator for Draft, Pending, Finalized, or Paid.

7.5 Auto-Billing

When configured in Settings, the auto-billing system automatically generates draft invoices from unbilled time entries.

  • Automatic trigger — Runs when you load the Dashboard if billing cycle conditions are met.
  • Manual trigger — “Run Now” button in the “Pending Auto-Billing” section on the Dashboard.
  • Scheduled trigger — A cron job runs weekly on Mondays at 6 AM.
  • Generated invoices appear as Draft with an “Auto-Generated” badge and billing period dates.
  • Review, edit, and approve auto-generated invoices like any other invoice.
  • Per-matter billing cycle overrides are respected if configured.

8 AI Features

The AI Text Assistant is available on textareas across the platform — notes, descriptions, invoice terms, firm address, and more. Look for the gold sparkle button at the bottom-right of supported textareas.

1
Click the gold sparkle button at the bottom-right of any supported textarea. A popover opens.

Choose a quick action: Fix Grammar, Formal, Concise, or Expand.
3
Or type a custom instruction, e.g., “Rewrite this for a corporate client” or “Make this more detailed.” Press Enter or click Go.
4
The AI streams the result in real time. Review the generated text.
5
Click Apply to Text to replace the textarea content with the AI-generated version.

8.2 AI Time Logger

Described in Section 5.3. The AI Time Logger at /time/ai-logger parses natural language descriptions into structured time entries automatically.

8.3 AI Insights (Dashboard)

The AI Insights panel on the Dashboard provides automated analysis and suggestions:

  • Call-to-action items with priority levels (High, Medium, Low).
  • Categorized as Billing, Compliance, Matters, Time Tracking, or General.
  • Click Refresh to generate new insights based on current firm data.

8.4 AI Agent Panel

A floating sparkle button is always available at the bottom-right of the screen. Click it to open the AI Agent Panel — an on-demand assistant that can answer questions about your firm’s data, suggest actions, and provide analysis.

Privacy: All AI features use Groq’s Llama 3.1 8B model. No client data is used for model training. Responses are generated in real time and are not stored.

Workflow Tips

  • Set up your firm profile first. Configure your firm name, logo, default rate, and address before creating any data. These propagate to all invoices and documents.
  • Use AI for descriptions. The AI Text Assistant is available on nearly every textarea. Use “Formal” for professional billing descriptions and “Fix Grammar” for quick proofreading.
  • Log time daily. Use the AI Time Logger for the fastest entry. Daily logging prevents forgotten billable hours and makes auto-billing more accurate.
  • Set up rate cards early. Configure client-specific rates before creating matters. The rate resolution cascade (matter → client → default) ensures the right rate is always applied.
  • Configure billing cycles. Set your billing cycle days and lead time in Firm Profile. Auto-billing will generate draft invoices automatically, saving hours of manual work each month.
  • Use guided tours. First time on a page? Click “Explore this screen?” for a walkthrough. Replay tours anytime from the Help button.
  • Import clients in bulk. When migrating from another system, download the CSV template, map your data, and import hundreds of clients at once.
